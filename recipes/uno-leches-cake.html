Uno Leches Cake

Home Brisket Soy Sauce Eggs

This recipe will teach you how to make an "Uno Leches cake," a take on the popular "Tres Leches" dish.

Ingredients

  • flour

  • eggs

  • milk

  • baking powder

  • vanilla extract

  • butter

  1. mix dry ingredients

  2. mix wet ingredients in

  3. bake cake at 350 for about 30 mins

  4. soak in milk

  5. let sit in refrigerator overnight

  6. enjoy!
